Platform Features

Everything a school payment process needs — in one place

From the first fee notice to the final receipt, EduPay keeps every step traceable and every record consistent.

Online Payments

Make full or permitted partial payments across multiple simulated channels — card, bank transfer, USSD, or gateway checkout.

Digital Receipts

Every confirmed payment generates a unique, printable A4 receipt linked to its transaction — downloadable and verifiable.

Payment History

Search, filter, and sort every transaction by status, method, session, and student — with CSV export of permitted records.

Outstanding Balances

Balances update only after a confirmed success, so pending or failed attempts never inflate what a student owes.

Parent & Student Accounts

Parents see only the students linked to them, and can switch between linked children to pay and view receipts.

Finance Administration

Manage fee structures, sessions, classes, deadlines, and payment plans with full audit logging of every change.

Transaction Verification

Finance officers review pending and offline submissions, then approve, reject, or request more information — with reasons recorded.

Reporting & Reconciliation

Collections by period, category, class, and method — plus bank settlement matching that flags unmatched or duplicate records.

Built for trust — and honest about its limits

This build is a frontend MVP. It never stores card numbers, CVVs, PINs, or banking passwords, and it labels every simulated payment as such. Going live means moving the trust boundary to a server.

Demonstration notice: payments in this build are simulated and are not processed securely. A production deployment requires server-side authentication, provider-verified payment confirmation, and tamper-resistant audit records.
  • Role-based access — financial data is restricted by role, and knowing a student ID never grants access.
  • Balances change only on confirmed success; pending, failed, and cancelled transactions never count as paid.
  • Receipts are generated only from confirmed records, each with a unique number tied to its transaction.
  • Every sensitive action — login, fee change, verification, export — is written to an audit log.
  • Production path: backend validation, idempotent payment recording, webhook signature checks, and HTTPS sessions.
